A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you
get started.

Can I request a specific make or model?+

Yes. Add your preferred make, model, year and any must-have features to the enquiry builder. Sourcing is subject to availability.

What is included in an import price?+

Request an itemised quote covering the vehicle, sourcing fees, inland transport, export documents, freight and insurance. Destination duties, taxes and registration should be confirmed separately unless explicitly included.

Can any vehicle be imported into my country?+

Eligibility depends on local rules, including vehicle age, emissions, steering position and inspection requirements. Confirm these before purchase.

How long does importing take?+

Timing depends on finding the vehicle, documentation, vessel schedules and customs clearance. Ask for a route-specific estimate before making a commitment.
